A little about me

Hello, my name is Valeria, and welcome to my portfolio. I am currently a freshman at Arizona State University in the Fulton Schools of Engineering, studying UX research and design. I am originally from Russia, lived in Texas before eventually moving to Florida. My background is a little different from the traditional tech path, which is actually part of what led me to UX. I became interested in understanding how people interact with technology, what frustrates them, and how design can make those experiences easier and more intuitive. I am still early in my UX journey, and this portfolio is a place for me to share what I am learning and the projects I create along the way. In my free time, I like to spend time with my parrots.
